The embedded host fires a listen by the check's plan of it. `dispatch(tenant, { adapterType, connection?, arrivals })` takes what a module reports arrived, where it took an event; each arrival runs every saved listen on its module and last edge that hears its connection and whose judge (the engine's, as listen-fire's) says it fires. `SavedProgram.listens` (`ListenPlan[]`) replaces `listeners`. `run` lands an Invocation on the manual unit's `Invocations`. A save refuses, by name, a listen in the retired settings form, one whose earlier hop only listen-fire lists, and one that states the node its landing is. An arrival the judge cannot decide runs nothing and is said through the new `log` option. `Arrival` is re-exported.
