A module states how a listen learns a hop's members changed in one member, `membersChange: "pushed" | { relistEverySeconds }`, where it stated `cheapToList` with `parentChanges` or `relistEverySeconds`. A field of kind `owned` may declare `format` (`cron` or `timeZone`), and the checker validates a literal by that format alone, never by the scalar's name. A field may declare `changedIn: { release, note }`, so a check for an upgrade from before that release warns where a program reads it. A `listenSettingsBecome` mapping may declare `changed` (the rewrite hears otherwise, said and never applied safely) and `alsoArrivesOn` (its events arrive on more edges: no rewrite), and a key's `values` may spell a value `null` (it narrowed nothing).
