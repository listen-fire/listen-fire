A Papermark link is read as a PDF of its pages, one screenshot each, as a DocSend deck is. The crawler waits for the gate or the viewer instead of a fixed six seconds, fills an email or password gate and submits once Continue enables, then turns through the horizontal viewer or scrolls the vertical one, waiting for each page's image to load. A page it does not recognise goes to the AI fallback instead of failing with "Failed to authenticate". It refuses, saying which, the gates a crawler must not push through: an NDA, a one-time code sent to an inbox, and an email gate with no email given.
