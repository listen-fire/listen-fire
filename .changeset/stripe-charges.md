`@lf/stripe` reads charges and says why a payment failed. A `Charge` is one attempt to take a payment, failed ones included: `Amount`, `Amount Captured` and `Amount Refunded` in major units, `Status` (`succeeded`, `pending` or `failed`), `Paid`, `Refunded`, `Created`, `Failure Code` and `Failure Message` (Stripe's reason, written for the customer), the `outcome` object as `Outcome Type`, `Outcome Reason`, `Outcome Seller Message` (Stripe's reason, written for you), `Outcome Network Status`, `Outcome Network Decline Code`, `Outcome Advice Code` and `Outcome Risk Level`, `Receipt URL`, `Billing Details Name` and `Email`, and `Metadata`. The account has `Charges` (newest first), a customer has `Charges`, and a charge leads to its `Customer` and `Invoice`. `Created` bounds in a `Charges` WHERE (`>=`, `<`, `WITHIN 7d`) reach Stripe's listing, which paginates; Stripe cannot filter charges by status, so `Status` runs over what comes back. An invoice's `Charges` are every attempt to pay it, newest first, so a failed invoice's reason is `only(inv-[c:Charges WHERE c.Status == "failed" ORDER BY c.Created DESC LIMIT 1]->)`: since API version 2025-03-31.basil an invoice names no charge and a charge no invoice, so they are joined through Stripe's invoice payments and the payment intent's charges. The `charge.captured`, `.expired`, `.failed`, `.pending`, `.refunded`, `.succeeded` and `.updated` events are typed, and an Event leads to its `Charge`; a `charge.*` event about a dispute or a refund is still answered 200 and runs nothing. A restricted key now needs Read on Charges too. Still nothing is written to Stripe.
