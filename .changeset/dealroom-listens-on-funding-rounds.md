Dealroom funding rounds arrive on the `Funding Rounds` edge, readable and listenable, polled every ten minutes (lf.yaml `pollEvery` overrides it for a connection): ``listen to dealroom-[r:`Funding Rounds` WHERE r.Round IN ["SERIES A"] AND r.`Company Locations` CONTAINS "Germany"]-> fire f``. Each round Dealroom records after the listen began arrives once, with `occurredAt` and `about` the company that raised it; a round corrected afterwards does not arrive again. Two new fields, `Company Industries` and `Company Locations` (the industries, and the cities and countries, of the company that raised), are owned scalars Dealroom judges (`DealroomIndustry`, `DealroomLocation`: its vocabulary has regions a round never lists) and take `contains` and `containsAny` (an OR of CONTAINS, any of several); they and `Round` reach Dealroom's own terms filters, on a walk of `Funding Rounds` as on a listen, and the module judges the rest of the taken filter itself. A walk asks an OR of terms one term at a time, and each round carries the terms it was found by, so the engine's own check of the WHERE agrees; two such conjuncts on one field fail the walk by name, since Dealroom matches any of a field's terms, never all. A round has an `Id`, its identity. It declares how a 0.12 `listen to dealroom { rounds, industries, hq_locations }`, with `events: ["funding_round"]` or without, becomes this (`listenSettingsBecome`) and no longer declares `listenConfig`, a `fires` edge or event types. Adapter protocol 2.6.
