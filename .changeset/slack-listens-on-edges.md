Slack is listened to on the edge each record arrives on, each landing on its own record type: a channel's `Messages`, a message's `Reactions` and its `Interactions` (``listen to chat-[ch:Channels WHERE ch.Name IN ["deals"]]->-[m:Messages WHERE m.`Mentions App` == true]-> fire f``). A message and a reaction arrive carrying their channel (named from the cached channel read), a press carrying its channel and the message pressed, so a listen's filter on them is judged on the arrival with nothing listed; a reaction does not carry its message. A message written with `Buttons` may carry a `Topic`, stored as the id of the block its buttons sit in (`lf:<topic>`) and read back from there, so ``listen to chat-[:Channels]->-[m:Messages WHERE m.Topic == "lunch-poll"]->-[:Interactions]-> fire f`` runs once per press on any message that has it; a `Topic` without `Buttons` is refused by name. A message gains `Id` (`channel:ts`), `Topic` and `Mentions App`; a press walks to its `Message`; a message a run writes is handed `channel:ts` as its id. A delivery's lookups (the channel's name, the app's bot user) are bounded and never hold the delivery (`SLACK_LOOKUP_TIMEOUT_MS`, `SLACK_LOOKUP_RETRIES`, `SLACK_LOOKUP_FAILURE_CACHE_MS`; README). Block ids starting `lf:` are reserved for a `Topic`. Adapter protocol 2.6. It declares how a 0.12 `listen to chat { channels, events }` becomes a traversal (`listenSettingsBecome`). `listEventTypes`, the `fires` edges, `subscribableEvents`, `listenConfig` and `slackInboundEvents` are gone: `preprocessInbound` answers `arrivals`, no events.
