The index lists the sections of each `system:` chapter (`system:slack (sections: shapes-of-post, buttons-and-a-press, …)`), as the intents already route into the hand-written chapters' sections. A reader after one rule of a system, such as how to post to a channel, reads that section (`system:slack#shapes-of-post`) instead of the whole chapter. The micro examples answer "stripe payment failed" (a listen on a failed payment, reading its invoice) and "call an mcp tool" (a tool's answer read off the connection).
